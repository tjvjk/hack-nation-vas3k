## Video direction

Blue Professional design system remixed to the product's dark-navy, mint, and periwinkle tokens. Use a warm-light technical canvas with mint as the only focal accent, thin structural rules, and tinted panels without shadows. Each scene is a different architecture view, not a repeated card grid. Motion is diagrammatic and seek-safe: connections draw, packets travel, and result states settle. All visual content stays above the 83% caption keep-out band, even though captions are disabled.
